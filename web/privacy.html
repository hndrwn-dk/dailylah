Privacy Policy

Draft — review before publishing. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places (home, work, school), favourite bus stops, MRT lines and hawker centres are stored only on your device. DailyLah has no accounts and does not send your location to our servers.

What our server receives

When you look up a bus stop, the app sends that bus stop code to our API to fetch arrival times. Push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging topics (for example an MRT line), so our server knows topics, not who you are.

Crash reports

If enabled, crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics without location data. You can turn this off in Settings.

Data sources

Transport and environment data come from LTA DataMall and data.gov.sg. DailyLah is not affiliated with the Singapore Government.
