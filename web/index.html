Questions

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet: DailyLah is for Android 10 and newer.

Why is it a beta?

DailyLah is in closed testing on Google Play before its public release, so you join the testers group first.

Is it free?

Yes, DailyLah is free, with no ads and no account.
