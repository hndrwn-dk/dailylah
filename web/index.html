How we handle your data

Questions

Getting DailyLah

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet: DailyLah is for Android 10 and newer.

Why is it a beta?

DailyLah is in closed testing on Google Play before its public release, so you join the testers group first.

Is it free?

Yes, DailyLah is free, with no ads and no account.

Where does the information come from?

Public data from LTA DataMall, data.gov.sg, OneMap and URA. DailyLah is not affiliated with the Singapore Government. When a live reading is old, the app shows the time it was last updated.

Privacy and safety

Does DailyLah track me?

No. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no device ID is sent to us. For live bus times the app sends only the bus stop code, never where you are. Like any web service, our hosting keeps short-lived request logs (IP address and time), which we do not use to identify you.

When does it use my location?

Only when you tap Here, “Nearest stops to my location” or “Use my current location”. Your position is used on the phone to find what is around you, then discarded. It is not stored and not sent to us. You can say no and still pick a saved place or search an address.

Does it follow me in the background?

Only while Wake me at my stop or I’m on the train is running. You see an ongoing notification, and it stops when the ride ends. Your position is compared with your stop on the phone and is never stored or sent.

What does your server see?

Only what a request needs: bus stop codes, MRT line codes and the address text you search (passed to OneMap and not logged). Never your saved places, your home or work, or your GPS position.

Is my data backed up to Google?

No. DailyLah turns off Android backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so your places and history stay on this phone only. To set up another phone, use Share with family: a QR code with your bus stops, MRT lines and times that you show or send yourself.

How do I delete my data?

Trips, meals and parking you logged: More > Extras > Delete my history. Everything else: clear the app’s storage or uninstall. With no account, there is nothing to delete on our side.

What permissions does it ask for?

Location (only when you use it) and notifications. No contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Using it

How do alerts work without an account?

Your phone checks the latest public data about every 15 minutes and decides itself: rain before you leave, MRT disruptions, your hawker closed tomorrow, your bus to catch. The notification is made on your phone; no push service is involved. Turn alerts off any time in the app or in Android settings.

Will it drain my battery?

The 15-minute check is short and run by Android. GPS is used only while Wake me at my stop or I’m on the train is on, and stops when the ride ends.

Does it work without internet?

Your saved places, the downloaded public data (hawker centres, schools, parks, car parks and more) and the last live readings stay on your phone. Live bus times and train alerts need a connection.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Email support@tursinalabs.com, or tap Contact us in the app’s About page.
