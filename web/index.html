How we handle your data

Questions

Getting DailyLah

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet: DailyLah is for Android 10 and newer.

Where do I get it?

On Google Play, for phones with Android 10 or newer.

Is it free?

Yes, DailyLah is free, with no ads and no account.

Where does the information come from?

Public data from LTA DataMall, data.gov.sg, OneMap and URA. DailyLah is not affiliated with the Singapore Government. When a live reading is old, the app shows the time it was last updated.

Privacy and safety

Does DailyLah track me?

No. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no device ID is sent to us. For live bus times the app sends only the bus stop code, never where you are. Like any web service, our hosting keeps short-lived request logs (IP address and time), which we do not use to identify you.

When does it use my location?

It asks only when you tap Current location, “Nearest stops to my location” or “Use my current location”, or start Wake me at my stop or the bell of a bus you are waiting for. Once you have allowed it, the app also checks when you open it whether you are at your saved Home, Work or School, to show a greeting instead of your trip. Your position is used on the phone to find what is around you, then discarded. It is not stored and not sent to us. A trip you time from where you are is listed in History, on your phone, under the name of the nearest station or bus stop, not your position. If you save a place with “Use my current location”, that point is kept on your phone as the place’s address, like one you searched. You can say no and still pick a saved place or search an address.

Does it follow me in the background?

Only while Wake me at my stop, I’m on the train or Bus arriving (the bus of a trip you are timing) is running. You see an ongoing notification, and it stops when the bus has come or the ride ends. Your position is compared with your stop on the phone and is never stored or sent.

What does the app send to get live data?

Live bus times, crowd levels and address search come from public sources such as LTA and OneMap. To ask for them, the app sends only the bus stop code, the MRT line code or the address you search (not logged), and, if you use them, the postal code of an EV charging location or the registration number of a property agent you check. When you pick Car, Motorbike, Bicycle or Taxi for a trip, it also sends that trip’s destination (for a taxi, its start) as a rounded point, not logged, to find parking or taxis near it. Never where you are, and never your saved places, home or work unless one is the end of that trip.

Is my data backed up to Google?

No. DailyLah turns off Android backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so your places and history stay on this phone only. To set up another phone, use Share with family: a QR code with your bus stops, MRT lines and times that you show or send yourself.

How do I delete my data?

Trips, meals and parking you logged: History > Delete all my history. Everything else: clear the app’s storage or uninstall. With no account, there is nothing to delete on our side.

What permissions does it ask for?

Location (only when you use it) and notifications. No contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Using it

How do alerts work without an account?

Your phone checks the latest public data about every 15 minutes and decides itself: rain before you leave, MRT disruptions, your hawker closed tomorrow, your bus to catch. The notification is made on your phone; no push service is involved. Turn alerts off any time in the app or in Android settings.

How does DailyLah know about MRT disruptions?

Train alerts are based on LTA’s published service alerts. They cover the disruptions LTA announces, not every short delay, and may lag official announcements. DailyLah is not an official LTA, SMRT or SBS Transit channel.

Will it drain my battery?

The 15-minute check is short and run by Android. GPS is used only while Wake me at my stop, I’m on the train or Bus arriving is on, and stops when the bus has come or the ride ends.

Does it work without internet?

Your saved places, the downloaded public data (hawker centres, schools, parks, car parks and more) and the last live readings stay on your phone. Live bus times and train alerts need a connection.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Email support@tursinalabs.com, or tap Contact us in the app’s About page.
