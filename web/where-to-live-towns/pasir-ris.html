Living in Pasir Ris

From Pasir Ris MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 32 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 17th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Changi Business Park is the closest at 21 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 23 hours a month on the road and about $99 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 4-room flat, $3,400 a month. Compared with Tampines, 2.1 km away: the same time to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,400 a month (Q3 2026), $100 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $630,000 (Q2 2026), $13,450 below the median of 24 towns.
