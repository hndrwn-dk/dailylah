Living in Jurong West

From Boon Lay MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 33 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 19th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Jurong East is the closest at 10 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 24 hours a month on the road and about $100 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,700 a month. Compared with Jurong East, 4.1 km away: 6 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,600 a month (Q3 2026), $100 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $530,000 (Q2 2026), $113,450 below the median of 24 towns.
