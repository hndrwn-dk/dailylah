Living in Marine Parade

From Marine Parade MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 28 minutes away on a weekday morning with 2 transfers, the 14th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 15 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 21 hours a month on the road and about $82 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,000 a month. Compared with Geylang, 3.0 km away: 17 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $300 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,500 a month (Q3 2026), the same as the median of 25 towns. The 3-room median resale price is $480,000 (Q3 2025), $43,050 above the median of 22 towns.
