Living in Toa Payoh

From Toa Payoh MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 15 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 5th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, one-north is the closest at 25 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 11 hours a month on the road and about $74 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,000 a month. Compared with Bishan, 2.0 km away: 3 minutes shorter to the CBD and 4-room rent $150 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,850 a month (Q3 2026), $350 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $1,019,000 (Q2 2026), $375,550 above the median of 24 towns.
