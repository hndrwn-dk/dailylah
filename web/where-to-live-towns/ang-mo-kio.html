Living in Ang Mo Kio

From Ang Mo Kio MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 22 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 9th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Woodlands is the closest at 28 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 16 hours a month on the road and about $85 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,800 a month. Compared with Bishan, 2.1 km away: 4 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $300 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,400 a month (Q3 2026), $100 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $638,000 (Q2 2026), $5,450 below the median of 24 towns.
