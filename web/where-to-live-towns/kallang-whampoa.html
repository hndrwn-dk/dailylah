Living in Kallang/Whampoa

From Boon Keng MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 15 minutes away on a weekday morning with 1 transfer, the 5th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 20 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 11 hours a month on the road and about $66 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,000 a month. Compared with Toa Payoh, 2.2 km away: the same time to the CBD and 4-room rent $50 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,800 a month (Q3 2026), $300 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $918,900 (Q2 2026), $275,450 above the median of 24 towns.
