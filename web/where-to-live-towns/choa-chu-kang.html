Living in Choa Chu Kang

From Choa Chu Kang MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 38 minutes away on a weekday morning with 1 transfer, the 23rd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Jurong East is the closest at 12 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 28 hours a month on the road and about $100 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,700 a month. Compared with Bukit Panjang, 2.1 km away: the same time to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,100 a month (Q3 2026), $400 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $560,000 (Q2 2026), $83,450 below the median of 24 towns.
