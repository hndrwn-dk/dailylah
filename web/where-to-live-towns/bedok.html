Living in Bedok

From Bedok MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 20 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 8th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 9 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 15 hours a month on the road and about $84 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,800 a month. Compared with Marine Parade, 3.4 km away: 8 minutes shorter to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,400 a month (Q3 2026), $100 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $582,500 (Q2 2026), $60,950 below the median of 24 towns.
