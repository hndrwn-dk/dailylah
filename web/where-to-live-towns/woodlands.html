Living in Woodlands

From Woodlands MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 48 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the slowest of 26 HDB towns. It is itself the Woodlands office area. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 35 hours a month on the road and about $105 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 2-room flat, $2,230 a month. Compared with Sembawang, 3.9 km away: 10 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,100 a month (Q3 2026), $400 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $550,000 (Q2 2026), $93,450 below the median of 24 towns.
