Living in Bukit Merah

From Redhill MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 9 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 2nd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, one-north is the closest at 14 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 7 hours a month on the road and about $66 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 2-room flat, $2,650 a month. Compared with Queenstown, 1.4 km away: 2 minutes shorter to the CBD and 4-room rent $300 lower. The 4-room median rent is $4,000 a month (Q3 2026), $500 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $944,000 (Q2 2026), $300,550 above the median of 24 towns.
