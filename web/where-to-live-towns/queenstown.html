Living in Queenstown

From Queenstown MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 11 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 3rd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, one-north is the closest at 11 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 8 hours a month on the road and about $74 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,100 a month. Compared with Bukit Merah, 1.4 km away: 2 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $300 higher. The 4-room median rent is $4,300 a month (Q3 2026), $800 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $1,035,000 (Q2 2026), $391,550 above the median of 24 towns.
