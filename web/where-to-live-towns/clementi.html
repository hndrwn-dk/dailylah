Living in Clementi

From Clementi MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 23 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 10th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Jurong East is the closest at 9 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 17 hours a month on the road and about $85 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,100 a month. Compared with Bukit Timah, 3.1 km away: 4 minutes shorter to the CBD. The 4-room median rent is $4,100 a month (Q3 2026), $600 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $888,900 (Q2 2026), $245,450 above the median of 24 towns.
