Living in Central Area

From Tanjong Pagar MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 3 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 16 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 2 hours a month on the road and about $56 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,300 a month. Compared with Bukit Merah, 3.6 km away: 6 minutes shorter to the CBD and 4-room rent $800 higher. The 4-room median rent is $4,800 a month (Q3 2026), $1,300 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $1,194,400 (Q2 2026), $550,950 above the median of 24 towns.
