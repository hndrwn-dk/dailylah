Living in Geylang

From Aljunied MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 11 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 3rd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 3 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 8 hours a month on the road.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,900 a month. Compared with Kallang/Whampoa, 2.4 km away: 4 minutes shorter to the CBD and the same 4-room rent. The 4-room median rent is $3,800 a month (Q3 2026), $300 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $710,000 (Q2 2026), $66,550 above the median of 24 towns.
