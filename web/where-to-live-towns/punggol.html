Living in Punggol

From Punggol MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 36 minutes away on a weekday morning with 1 transfer, the 22nd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 26 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 26 hours a month on the road and about $93 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 2-room flat, $2,350 a month. Compared with Sengkang, 1.6 km away: 5 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $50 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,250 a month (Q3 2026), $250 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $680,000 (Q2 2026), $36,550 above the median of 24 towns.
