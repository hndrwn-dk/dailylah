Living in Sembawang

From Sembawang MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 38 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 23rd quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Woodlands is the closest at 10 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 28 hours a month on the road.

The lowest median rent here is for a 2-room flat, $2,350 a month. Compared with Yishun, 2.7 km away: 4 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,200 a month (Q3 2026), $300 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $595,000 (Q2 2026), $48,450 below the median of 24 towns.
