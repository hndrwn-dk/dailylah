Living in Bishan

From Bishan MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 18 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 7th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Paya Lebar is the closest at 18 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 13 hours a month on the road.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $3,000 a month. Compared with Toa Payoh, 2.0 km away: 3 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $150 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,700 a month (Q3 2026), $200 above the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $788,000 (Q2 2026), $144,550 above the median of 24 towns.
