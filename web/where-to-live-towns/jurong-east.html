Living in Jurong East

From Jurong East MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 27 minutes away on a weekday morning with no transfer, the 12th quickest of 26 HDB towns. It is itself the Jurong East office area. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 20 hours a month on the road and about $91 in fares.

The lowest median rent here is for a 3-room flat, $2,850 a month. Compared with Bukit Batok, 2.0 km away: 6 minutes shorter to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 higher. The 4-room median rent is $3,500 a month (Q3 2026), the same as the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $559,400 (Q2 2026), $84,050 below the median of 24 towns.
