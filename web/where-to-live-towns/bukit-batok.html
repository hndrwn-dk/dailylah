Living in Bukit Batok

From Bukit Batok MRT, CBD (Raffles Place) is 33 minutes away on a weekday morning with 1 transfer, the 19th quickest of 26 HDB towns. Of the other office areas, Jurong East is the closest at 6 minutes. A weekday commute to CBD (Raffles Place) adds up to about 24 hours a month on the road.

The lowest median rent here is for a 2-room flat, $2,500 a month. Compared with Jurong East, 2.0 km away: 6 minutes longer to the CBD and 4-room rent $100 lower. The 4-room median rent is $3,400 a month (Q3 2026), $100 below the median of 25 towns. The 4-room median resale price is $645,900 (Q2 2026), $2,450 above the median of 24 towns.
