## 2. The app
DailyLah is a free app that shows bus arrivals, MRT/LRT service status, platform crowd levels and weather forecasts for Singapore, and can notify you about MRT disruptions and rain before you leave. There is no account. Features may be added, changed or removed at any time.

## 3. Public data and accuracy
DailyLah displays information from public sources, mainly LTA DataMall and data.gov.sg (including NEA data). Bus arrival times, crowd levels and forecasts are **estimates** and can be late, incomplete or wrong. Notifications can be delayed or missed, for example when your phone is offline or Android limits background activity to save battery.

Use DailyLah as a convenience only. Always follow official announcements, station signage and staff instructions. Do not rely on DailyLah for safety-critical or emergency decisions.

Those sources and their providers are not responsible for how DailyLah presents the data.

Sources, each used under its own open data terms:
- data.gov.sg: datasets from NEA (weather, hawker centres, dengue), HDB (blocks, resale and rental figures, including the quarterly median rent and resale price on the Where to live? page), CEA (estate agents), MUIS (prayer times), NParks (parks), NLB (libraries), SportSG (sports facilities), PA (community clubs), MOE (schools), SCDF (AEDs, fire stations, shelters), MOH (CHAS clinics) and MOM (public holidays).

Toilets may be closed, moved or inside paid areas.
- Eventfinda: events near home at the weekend. Check details and tickets on the event's page.
- Muslim.sg: halal listings. DailyLah is not affiliated with MUIS or Muslim.sg; always verify halal status directly with the outlet or MUIS.
