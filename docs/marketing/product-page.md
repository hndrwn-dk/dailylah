# DailyLah

Know before you go lah. Buses, MRT and rain, before you leave the house.

[Google Play badge]

## What it does

- Shows one morning card and one evening card with what matters for your commute today.
- Lists the next buses at your saved stops, with how crowded each bus is.
- Tells you when the MRT or LRT line you use has a disruption, including free bus and shuttle info.
- Warns you about rain in the hour before you usually leave, at home or at the office.
- Shows how crowded MRT platforms are on your evening card, so you can choose when to head home.

[Feature graphic]

## Who it's for

- People in Singapore who take the bus and MRT every day.
- Parents doing school runs who want to know about rain before stepping out.
- Anyone who wants one simple daily summary instead of checking several apps.

## Key Features

- Morning and evening cards
- Live bus arrivals and bus load
- MRT/LRT disruption alerts
- Rain alerts before you leave
- Platform crowd levels
- Search any bus stop, offline list
- Home screen widget
- Quiet hours, max 3 rain alerts a day
- No account, locations stay on your phone
- Free

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your saved places, lines and settings stay on your phone.

### Does DailyLah track my location?
No. Location is used only when you tap "nearest stops", once, on your phone. You can skip it and search stops by name or code instead.

### Where does the data come from?
From public sources: LTA DataMall for buses and trains, and data.gov.sg for NEA weather. DailyLah is not an official government app.

### Will it spam me with notifications?
No. You only get alerts for your own MRT lines and for rain near your usual departure time, never between 10 pm and 6 am, and at most 3 rain alerts a day. Each alert can be turned off.

### Is it free?
Yes. DailyLah is free, with no ads.
